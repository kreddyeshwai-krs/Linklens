⚠️ Safety & Heuristic Disclaimer

Link Lens is an educational cybersecurity simulator. Risk scores are heuristic indicators based on syntactic patterns, domain registration structure, and configured intelligence feeds. A low score indicates "no major red flags detected" rather than a guaranteed safe destination. Link Lens never opens submitted URLs in your browser and never executes arbitrary remote scripts.
